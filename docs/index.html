Join in!

Civic Volunteerism is a highly-visible way to elevate our communities, but we also enable volunteers to gain valuable job skills and new items on their CVs that are useful during a job search or pursuit of higher education. Open source development is a learning opportunity, a teaching opportunity, and a winning opportunity, for those involved and for those who reap the benefits of these endeavors. When you meet the people who benefit from your work, in your neighborhood, at your school or workplace, or maybe just out and about, you learn directly what it means to make things for others. This is true in the workaday world, to be sure, but it is not every day that you interact with the people you have helped with your language translations, visualizations, audio transformations, human interfaces, and applications. Here, you can!

General Information

About Us

Some history, the Code for Vegas Foundation was rebooted from the original Code for Vegas in 2022, formed as an independent Nevada Nonprofit Corporation on 16 June 2022, and is a 501(c)(3) public charity determined by the IRS, effective retroactively from the time of our formation. While we had kept the name of our predecessor organization formed in 2014, we endeavor to include all comers wishing to contribute and collaborate on these civic projects and goals, without a specific focus on coding. Thus our legal name change to Innovate for Vegas Foundation, to be more inclusive of volunteers with a diverse set of skills and interests and to look forward, not backward…

Purpose

To enable and encourage ideation, innovation, and implementation to make our communities better.

This means focusing the energies of local and non-local innovators in communities near and far on innovation that Makes a Smart City Smarter while elevating communities regardless of locality.

Ethos

Our core principles are essential:

  • Inclusivity
  • Diversity
  • Accessibility

These principles apply not only to project participation, but also to all meetups, gatherings, online publications, project deployments, and ongoing development of any type of work product. We invite all those interested in learning, contributing, and collaborating on civic projects designed to improve our communities, to do so with confidence and conviction!

Mission

We will invite members of the population at large to build an organization centered on civic volunteerism, skills development, and job creation, with a focus on Making our Smart City Smarter, improving Education, and driving our 3-word ethos: Inclusivity, Diversity, Accessibility.

As is the case so often with Open Source Projects, all of our work is open to collaboration with others in the civic volunteer universe.

According to the Las Vegas Convention and Visitor Authority there are approximately 40 million people visiting our Oasis in the Desert for 2024 (give or take), and we would like very much for those visitors to try civic volunteer projects out for themselves! Governance

As a Nevada Nonprofit Corporation, the Innovate for Vegas Foundation is governed by a Board of Directors, made up of those interested in elevating communities through civic volunteerism, including but not limited to so-called Civic Tech pursuits.

Members of our board are not compensated (nor is anyone in our organization, 100% volunteer), and are not requested nor required to participate in the actual pursuit of community elevating initiatives, though they are certainly welcome to join in day-to-day. Our Board members help us make long term planning decisions, present-day policy decisions, and tell our story when out and about.

Directors need not be residents of the State of Nevada nor located in the Greater Vegas area. In fact, Directors need not be US Citizens nor US Residents. Directors serve a term of one year with an automatic option to renew for the following year, up to 20 consecutive years. If you see an empty seat or would like to see us add one, please reach out to our organization secretary via email (secretary at innovateforvegas.org).

Ongoing Initiatives

All Innovate for Vegas Foundation Initiatives are self-sponsored, designed and managed by Civic Volunteers within our organization. There are no end dates, which makes each Initiative and its Component Projects an ongoing development effort, which may be joined sooner or later. This means there is always something to work on, to improve, to be inspired by, especially for those new to Civic Volunteerism and Community Elevation.

Our overall strategy is to make an attractive appeal to Civic Volunteers interested in contributing their time, effort, and expertise to any of existing or new Initiatives, with the knowledge that their contributions will not be archived nor tossed out. Building components that fit together in a larger plan, one of our Apex Initiatives for example, will help to provide a roadmap to sustained use, in as much as we are able to keep contributed creative work actively integrated into one or more Component Parts of any Initiative.

Adding new Component Projects or whole Initiatives is an activity for a Whole Team, a part of the Agile for Volunteers Initiative driven by our Foundation for our own uses, and for public consumption and experimentation. Our organization is entirely open source, volunteer-driven, and open to collaboration with The World, for the purposes of Community Elevation, wherever and whichever those communities may be.

Apex Initiatives

Each Initiative focus is not only useful and valuable on its own, but each will come together as part of a larger Initiative with even more general utility than each of the individual Initiatives and Component Project which we pursue on an ongoing basis. Each Initiative and its Component Projects, and each Apex Initiative, will bring job skills development and, ideally, actual jobs to participants, whether that participation is in the form of software code development, graphics, human interface, media development, content creation, artistic works, internationalization and localization efforts, accessibility checks, quality assurance, and even marketing, sales, and project management. Each role is essential and each contributor gets credit for their effort from start to finish, as a matter of policy.

Virtual Vegas

Virtual Vegas is a Digital Twin implementation, making maximal use of Open Data datasets and all of the specific topics addressed with each Initiative, the goal is to enable the smartest of smart applications and other projects developments to enable discovery, learning, and participation in our Smart City. It would be easy to compare this project to visual simulation games like Civilization or Sim City, and both of those would be valid comparisons, but this Apex Initiative will aim to be a platform on which someone might build a game similar to these, or develop open news tools, neighborhood communications tools, safety visualizations, and so on. The possibilities are endless!

Education

This Apex Initiative is in search of direction, having stuck in the earliest phases of planning and discussion. Early thinking is to enable a more flexible education platform for students of all ages in the Las Vegas area (and beyond), adapting what was learned during school closures in pandemic lockdown intervals, adaptation to different learning styles, and enabling learning at different rates for different students, especially in cases where there might be a teacher staffing shortage. If Making our Smart City Smarter is the one goal of the Innovate for Vegas Foundation, Education and an educated populace are the bedrock foundation on which this goal is best built.
